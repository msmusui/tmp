TriPlanar Z-Blend Surface Shader layers snow, grass, moss, and dust on top-facing surfaces using slope, height, curvature, AO/cavity and world-space noise. Build natural accumulation on rocks and cliffs without UV seams, with scalable presets for different budgets.

  • Z+ accumulation Keep vertical faces rocky while top-facing surfaces accumulate snow, grass, or moss with clean art direction controls.
  • Slope / Height / Curvature Define snowlines, prevent build-up on steep slopes, and push growth into cracks and cavities using curvature.
  • World-space breakup Noise-driven breakup removes banding, adds natural patchiness, and helps large meshes feel grounded.
  • Wetness & cavity growth Drive moss saturation and roughness with wetness maps, cavity masks, and AO for believable damp areas.
